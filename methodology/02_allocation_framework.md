# 02 · Allocation framework

The allocation maps assumptions → target weights through a transparent, stated
process (not a black box):

1. **Universe** — the published asset classes (see the assumptions CSV).
2. **Objective** — a long-horizon risk-adjusted objective, stated in prose here;
   the exact optimizer settings stay private.
3. **Constraints** — published *types* of constraints (per-asset caps, no shorting in
   the long-only sleeve, liquidity floors) without the specific numeric bounds that
   would reveal the live book.
4. **Output** — target weights at the asset-class level, illustrative only.

The point of publishing this is to show the allocation is principled and repeatable.
The point of withholding the numeric constraints and the live weights is that those
*are* the position.

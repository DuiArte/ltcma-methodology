# 03 · Rebalancing rules

Rebalancing is rule-based and published as rules:

- **Trigger** — a tolerance-band rule (rebalance an asset class when it drifts beyond
  a band around target). The *existence* of bands is published; the exact band widths
  are withheld (they reveal turnover behavior and indirectly the live weights).
- **Cadence** — a maximum review frequency (the site refreshes daily; allocation
  review is far less frequent).
- **Cost awareness** — trades are netted and cost-screened before execution.

What stays private: the numeric band widths, the actual trade list, and any
execution detail tied to the broker account.
